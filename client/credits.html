Dust2

“de_dust2 - CS map” by vrchris, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, according to the supplied model metadata.

The original mesh has been converted for browser rendering, with coordinates normalized to metres. Thirty textures received AI-assisted detail restoration at higher resolution; original PNGs and the credit textures are retained. Collision, navigation, player spawns and bomb objectives were added for Breachpoint. Source and modifications.

Compound, Province, Italy, Assault and Office

These five maps were supplied through the map-assets release. Source geometry, UVs and material assignments are retained, with coordinates normalized for gameplay and textures resized and encoded as WebP. Italy, Assault and Office retain their baked lighting. Collision, navigation, player spawns and two bomb objectives were added for Breachpoint.

Assault also has an added steel access ramp at the warehouse loading lip so players and bots can reach the raised floor.
