Dust2

“de_dust2 - CS map” by vrchris, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, according to the supplied model metadata.

The original mesh and textures have been converted for browser rendering, with coordinates normalized to metres. Collision, navigation, player spawns and bomb objectives were added for Breachpoint.

Weapons and characters

Uploaded weapon models were normalized for game scale and grips, converted for browser rendering, and given optimized textures. The classic character meshes retain their source textures and now use an articulated skeleton for movement, aiming and crouching.
